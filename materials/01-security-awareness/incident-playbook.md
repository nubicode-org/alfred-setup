# My incident playbook

Fill this in before you need it. In the moment, follow it rather than improvising.

## If I clicked a link or opened an attachment I should not have

1. Disconnect from the network (Wi-Fi off, cable out). Do not shut the computer down; evidence lives in memory.
2. Tell: ___
3. From a different, trusted device, change the password of any account I typed into that page.
4. Write down what I clicked, when, and what happened, while I still remember.

## If an account is taken over

1. From a trusted device, use the provider's recovery process: ___
2. Sign out every other session and remove devices I do not recognize.
3. Change the password, then turn two-factor authentication back on with a new secret.
4. Check the account's forwarding rules, recovery email and connected apps for anything added.
5. Change the password anywhere else I used the same one.

## If my laptop or phone is lost or stolen

1. Locate, lock and, if needed, erase it: ___ (the "find my device" service)
2. Sign it out of my email and password manager from another device.
3. Report it to: ___

## If I receive a request that feels urgent

1. Stop. Urgency is the technique.
2. Verify through a channel I already trust: ___
3. If it was fake, report it to: ___
